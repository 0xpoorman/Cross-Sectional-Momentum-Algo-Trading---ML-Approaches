# Ranking storytelling

## When to use
Use after the evidence audit when the reader needs an honest progression story.

## Procedure
1. Start with the decision-useful answer and scope.
2. Explain why cross-sectional ranking was attempted and what one date-group represents.
3. Contrast baseline, disciplined search, stable selection, and final evidence.
4. Show ranking quality, Sharpe, return, and benchmark comparison as distinct questions.
5. Explain short-side, volatility, capacity, and exit-policy findings with denominators and caveats.
6. End with what generalizes, what does not, and the next evidence needed.

## Writing rules
Use candid senior-level prose. Never claim alpha, causality, superiority, or production readiness unless saved evidence supports it.
