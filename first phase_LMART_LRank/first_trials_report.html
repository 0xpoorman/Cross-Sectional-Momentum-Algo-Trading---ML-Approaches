Reproduction contract

  • LambdaMART: LightGBM lambdarank, full lists; truncation is not configured.
  • LambdaRank: neural two-tail NDCG-weighted pairwise objective with ReLU and LayerNorm; no ridge baseline.
  • Feature set: the original ten OHLCV-derived features, no MACD.
  • Labels: next-open to open three bars later; no take-profit or stop-loss.
  • Costs: zero, matching the early research simplification.
